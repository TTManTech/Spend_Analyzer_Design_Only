# Merchant logos

Shown next to a merchant in the "Top purchases" list of the Spend by category section.
Each brand has two files: `<brand>-light.svg` (light mode) and `<brand>-dark.svg` (dark mode).
The brand keys and the merchant names they match are listed in `BRANDS` in `index.html`;
a brand without files simply shows no logo, so adding a logo is just adding its two files.

Included now (icons from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), CC0; the logos themselves
are trademarks of their owners and are used here only to identify the merchant):
target, mcdonalds, starbucks, tacobell.

Not included (not available in an openly licensed set): costco, walmart, wholefoods, kroger,
jewelosco, 7eleven, pandaexpress, wendys, chipotle, dunkin, subway, chickfila, dairyqueen,
panera, wingstop, culvers, noodles, portillos, benjerrys, parisbaguette.
